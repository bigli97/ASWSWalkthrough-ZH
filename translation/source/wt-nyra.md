# Nyra

Cheiftain's wife at [Rumah](42-rumah.md). Dasan's mother.

1. After her husband gets killed there are different scenarios for her depending on your choice how to deal with Dasan. (See [Tia](09-tia.md) step 17).

2. Talk to her and tell her to make herself useful as a cook. She will agree to this.

3. To trigger the next step you must have sex with 3 of the 4 other women ([Ayita](46-ayita.md), [Tia](09-tia.md), [Hiba](44-hiba.md), [Raaisha](43-raaisha.md)) near the fire while she is cooking. See their routes and you should figure out how to do it.

4. After that talk to Nyra and ask for soup, after she gave you some ask for another bowl. She will offer a deal to give you a blowjob.

5. After she gave you 3 blowjobs (Even if you prevented her from getting corrupted with Lucius potion) talk to her when she isn't cooking. Depending on the route she is on she will offer to become your wanita.

6. Depending on location (morning, evening, night) a different animation will play out when having sex with her.

7. After she became your wanita talk to her again, from now on you will be able to sleep with her in the chieftains hut. If on Dasan's route at first you have to provoke him by talking to him (at late hour this will send make him go to bed).

8. The next day if talk to Nyra again and want her to participate in pleasing other tribes men you can now ask her to do that. (on Dasan's route he will have the say in it).

9. Winterhowl in Rumah: To trigger this event it must be either December ingame or in reallife (you can also change the clock on your pc). Visit Rumah after Nyra became your wanita, this will trigger a quest where you have to cut some fir trees (all trees that have needles). Visit her again and give her time to decorate. In The meantime visit Frisha to buy a dress for her. Return to Nyra with the dress. This will now also enable a new anal scene inside the Chieftains hut.

10. Should you visit Nyra in the chieftains hut with Tia in group she will stay and watch (She needs C15+ for that).
